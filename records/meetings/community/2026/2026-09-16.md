## Reducing per-row position delete index probes in the vectorized read path

Daehong explained that vectorized reads probe the position delete index once per row, although the positions in a batch are a contiguous ascending range, so the index can be traversed once instead. The gain is significant when few columns are projected and not visible end to end on wide projections, where the delete check is a small share of scan CPU. The change needs a new API, PositionDeleteIndex.forEachInRange, and he asked whether the community is fine with that. The notes record no answer.

**Open questions:**
- Is the community OK with adding the new PositionDeleteIndex.forEachInRange API?

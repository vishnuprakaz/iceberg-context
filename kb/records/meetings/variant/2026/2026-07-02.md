## Shredding inference benchmarks

The goal was the inference strategy that best balances file size and write speed. Four strategies were compared with an unshredded baseline on 100k-row workloads on a local machine: B1 Majority (current; shreds if a primitive type appears in more than 10% of a 100-row sample), V2 Uniform (only if the type is 100% uniform in the sample), V2 Wilson (adds a confidence interval, for example 90%, to the B1 check), and B20 Uniform (uniform with a 20-row sample). Wilson is the leading candidate: it avoids the file-size waste of B1 Majority on polymorphic data, with write times comparable to B1 and read times slightly better in some cases. Future runs will use larger real-world datasets such as Netflix data and ClickBench.

## Read benchmark rework

The current read benchmarks were called misleading because they measure Spark's expensive full-object re-serialization rather than core reader performance. New benchmarks will simulate direct column extraction, which is the key performance goal. Read performance will be re-evaluated after the reader improvements merge.

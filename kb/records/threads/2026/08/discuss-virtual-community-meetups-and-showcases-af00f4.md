Elizabeth Garrett Christensen said she organizes something similar for Postgres, had been thinking about virtual meetups for Iceberg, and shared a document with her notes after talking with Kevin Liu about how to start. Scott Haines supported the proposed format of 10 minutes of community announcements, 20 to 30 minutes of technical content and 15 minutes of open discussion, the monthly cadence and the strict no-marketing guidance.
